# Relevance

The hand label given to one judged passage: not relevant, partly relevant, or relevant.

**You provide:** `grade` (0, 1 or 2).

- Use it in the judging view and in any table of pooled judgments. It does not belong on a voter-facing result card.
- The grade is shown as two pips (none, one or both filled) and the words, in ink. It has no colour of its own, so it cannot be confused with a party or a method.
- Keep the wording exact: "Not relevant", "Partly relevant", "Relevant".
