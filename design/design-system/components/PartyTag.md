# PartyTag

A member's party as a letter and a word, tinted with the party colour.

**You provide:** `party` ("D", "R" or "I"), and `short` when only the letter fits (a dense table).

- This is the only place the party colours appear. Do not colour a card, a name, a row or a chart series by party.
- The letter is always present, so party never depends on colour alone.
- Give all three parties the same size and weight. Order them alphabetically (Democrat, Independent, Republican) wherever they are listed together.
- Members who caucus with a party but are elected as independents are "I".
