Which search works best?

Each method answers the same test questions. Its top 10 passages for every question are labelled by hand, then scored. Values appear here once the judgments are complete.

Scores by method

About 50 questions: about 20 that use the same words as the articles, about 20 that use different words, and 10 that include a filter. Recall@10 counts only passages that at least one method returned. A difference is reported as real only when a significance test supports it.

Filtering before or after the search

Filtering first searches only the passages that match the filter. Filtering after searches everything, keeps the top 10, 50 or 100, and discards what does not match, so fewer than 10 results may be left.
