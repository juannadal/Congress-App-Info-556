Three ways to search

Ranks passages by the words they share with your question, and counts rarer words for more. A search for “veteran” will not find “served in the Marine Corps”.

Compares what your question means with what each passage means, and returns the closest. It can find a passage that shares no words with the question.

Runs both and merges the two rankings, so a passage that does well in either one can rise to the top. This is the default.
